Privacy Policy — Golf Green Reader

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Golf Green Reader ("the app") is developed by Murat Atabey ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the app collects, why, and how you can have it deleted.

1. Account data

To use the app you sign in with your Google account (via Firebase Authentication). We receive your email address and basic profile information (name) to identify your account. We do not receive your Google password.

2. Data we store about your use of the app

Tied to your account, in our database (Google Firestore), we store:

  • How many of your lifetime free hole-views you have used;
  • Your credit balance (purchased in-app);
  • Which courses and holes you have unlocked.

This data exists only to make the app work the same way across your devices and is never sold or shared for advertising.

3. Content you choose to send us

If you use the in-app "Report" (to flag outdated or incorrect course data) or "Send Request" (to ask for a new course or feature) option, we store the text you write, together with the course/hole it relates to and your account identifier, and we receive it by email so we can act on it.

4. Location

The app uses your device's GPS only locally, while you are using it, to help you find a course and position yourself on the green. We do not collect, transmit, or store your location on our servers.

5. Data kept only on your device

Your putting calibration, pinned hole/ball positions, favorite courses, and language preference are stored locally on your phone and are not sent to us.

6. In-app purchases

Purchases are processed entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment card or billing details — only a confirmation from Google that a purchase was made, which we use to add credits to your account.

7. Who else processes this data

  • Google Firebase / Google Cloud — authentication, database, backend functions, and file storage that power the app;
  • Google Play — payment processing for in-app purchases;
  • Resend — used only to deliver the emails generated by the Report/Send Request features described above.

8. How long we keep your data

We keep your account data for as long as your account exists, so your credits and progress stay available across devices. You can ask us to delete it at any time (see below).

9. Deleting your account and data

You can delete your account and all associated data in either of these ways:

  • In the app: open the main menu → Settings → Delete my account, and confirm. This removes your credits, unlocked courses/holes, and account record.
  • By email, without using the app: send a message to mratabey@gmail.com from the email address linked to your Google account, with the subject "Delete my Golf Green Reader account". We will delete your account data within 30 days and confirm by email.

Some information may be kept for a limited time where required for fraud prevention, accounting, or legal obligations (for example records of a payment), even after account deletion.

10. Your rights

If you are in the European Union, you have the right under the GDPR to access, correct, or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict its processing. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the email address below.

11. Children

This app is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

12. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the "last updated" date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the app.

13. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: mratabey@gmail.com.
