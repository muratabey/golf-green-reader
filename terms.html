1. The service

The app shows slope and elevation maps for golf greens in the Netherlands, built from public OSM course outlines and AHN elevation surveys, together with putt-aim suggestions based on calibration you perform yourself. The elevation data for each green is dated (shown in the app) — greens can be renovated or resurfaced after a survey was taken, so the data may not reflect the current state of a green. The app is a planning and practice aid; it is not a substitute for reading the green yourself.
